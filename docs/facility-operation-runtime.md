Existing09–17 Payroll remains unchanged. A separate explicit finite operation contract may cover0..7200 or another lawful shift; its actual wages derive only from performed operation in that original interval. Do not expand old Payroll hours or set personnel cost zero to bypass this dependency.

The first finite registration has at most4 immutable contracts and one Begin/Stop per contract. Each binds original Housing member range/population/version, employer and employee account, source device/role Electric or Water, exact venue offer and operation window, and cents per member-hour. Root checks original account-to-members mapping, explicit registered terms, qualifications and original venue activity. The original existing machine state and run intervals supply the physical witness; a caller's Boolean, nonzero device ID, payment or predicted work does not. Command ingress occurs at original completed boundaries; no later event rewrites already performed work. ## Original funds and obligations

Earned wage numerator is `hourly_cents_per_member * exact_member_count * actual_work_seconds`, with a retained denominator3600 remainder. Cumulative endpoints, not per-call rounding, define earned cents. Paid is progress of this original obligation, not cash created or held by a new wallet. Root transfers between original accounts once and confirms that same payment in the original record. Partial payment, stopping and restoration preserve earned-minus-paid plus the fractional entitlement; paying does not certify work.

For a new finite commitment root may reserve its maximum agreed wage in the existing Account.protected field, using the existing protect/pay_protected path. The contract's attributable protection is derived, not a second cash asset. Unrelated operations and generic Unprotect must leave at least the sum of existing mandatory protections and this contract's remaining funded obligation. After true close only unused future reservation is released; actual earned unpaid amounts remain protected/owed. Root must prove each release and prevent duplicate old Payroll/facility wages for the same employment window. Funding failure before acceptance backpressures; it cannot erase work already performed. Complete debt priorities and employment matching are not claimed by this finite gate.

Actual wages enter the particular device/purpose cost curve once. Maintenance and residual custody fees use separate original contracts, original recipients, bases and receipt times; neither is hidden in wages. Paying maintenance is not repaired equipment; paying custody is not disappearance/disposal of sludge. Quantity and lawful finite receiving responsibility remain.

## Original household income and explicit authority policy

Actual original cash payment, including an explicit later payment, adds only its actual checked paid delta to the existing household paid-wage window at the real payment tick. Earned amounts, protected funds, fractional entitlement and planned production are not income. The original 32-day ring and complete-day coverage rules are preserved; missing historical days are not synthesized. Existing Payroll and actual facility payments share this one window and one summary used by both the read-only income query and housing decisions. Stopped contracts still contribute actual pay in the queried period; qualifying/active contract counts follow their real accepted/settled intervals. No second wallet, wage window or employment clock is introduced.

The authority policy is explicitly version2: opening registration wraps `{authority_version:2, work:...}`, persisted runtime carries mandatory `version:2`, and receipt hashes have version2 domains. Original inputs with no facility registration remain compatible. Earlier experimental facility v1/unversioned openings/checkpoints are retained with their fixed programs; the new policy rejects them rather than silently inventing missing income history. This is a versioned behavior change, not a faster equivalent solver.

Cumulative actual paid counters only bound the window; they cannot prove a day was not deleted. Every real window change stamps a SHA256 of the complete original window, authority version and immutable facility binding into its original successful JournalRecord context. The fixed latest anchor contains its operation/revision and actual committed Timeline observation cursor. Actual payment day and a partially processed Timeline cursor are distinct: partial Payroll P3 payment must restore before P5 completion without moving money to another day. Raw structural loading remains unauthenticated until the matching original receipt verifies this hash. Rehashing a forged local window cannot substitute for the retained original ticket. A progressed facility world cannot delete both the window and its anchor to downgrade coverage; an empty pair is allowed only in a pristine state before any Timeline exists. Facility-enabled envelopes admit one original action so that receipt evidence remains unambiguous; old no-facility envelopes keep their existing rules.

Concrete counterexample source12 reached the original housing query after31 complete days: original cash had actually received4 cents, but income incorrectly returned `NoQualifiedPayroll/0`. Its source/program/checkpoint and actual values are preserved. Source16 core12 tests now pass, including actual4-cent complete-window income, mixed original Payroll+facility wages, explicit policy rejection and missing/double-deleted anchors. Store, broad shared regressions and final publication remain separate gates; these core results do not close #132 or its parents.
